Everything you need to read Chinese. On your own terms.

Bring anything you want to read or watch. JustShuo makes every word of it something you can look up, hear and learn.

Pinyin over every word

In tone colours, or hidden when you're ready. Simplified or traditional characters, whichever the text was written in, and fonts made for reading.

Click any word

Its meanings from a 125,000-word dictionary, its HSK level, the words it's part of, and a natural voice that says it, all without leaving the page.

Films and series

Open MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM, AVI or WMV videos with Chinese subtitles. Every line becomes clickable words with pinyin, the word being said lights up, and one key repeats a line.

Flashcards that work

Add a word or a whole sentence with one key. Reviews are spaced by FSRS, the scheduler serious learners use, and your cards export to Anki.

Know what you know

Words are coloured new, learning or known, and every text shows how much of it you can read, so you can choose what's right for you.

A dictionary that understands you

Look up English, Chinese or pinyin, with or without tones. Paste a sentence to see it word by word, with pinyin over each one.

Your progress at a glance

Words known and learning, your streak, reviews over the last weeks, and a rough HSK level, with your words in every level.

Bring your own

Private and offline
